# Editorial review checklist for clinic owner — DRAFT

Review each of the 30 bilingual articles in `content.js` against its named source links.

- [ ] Medical facts, severity language and scope fit current international guidance.
- [ ] Conditions, contraindications and risk flags are explicit; no DIY compression prescriptions.
- [ ] English and Arabic say the same clinically relevant thing.
- [ ] Emergency escalation is suitable for the country of operation.
- [ ] Content respects individual assessment and is not represented as guaranteed results.
- [ ] Professional reviewer name / credentials, approval date and next review due entered in a controlled register.

Avoid automated alteration of medical text after sign-off without a new clinician review.
